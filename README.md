# College Swimming Social Media Campaign

## Project Overview
A sample social media marketing campaign designed to
promote a college swim team and increase student engagement.

## Objectives
- Increase awareness of the swim team.
- Encourage attendance at home meets.
- Highlight athlete stories and team culture.

## Target Audience
College students, student-athletes, alumni, and swimming fans.

## Platform
Instagram: Reels, Stories, and feed posts.

## Content Strategy
- Monday: Athlete spotlight
- Tuesday: Behind the scenes
- Wednesday: Meet promotion
- Thursday: Team culture
- Friday: Meet day content

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Reach and impressions
- Engagement rate
- Story interactions
- Shares and saves
- Meet attendance, where measurable

## Tools
Canva, Instagram, Google Sheets, GitHub

## Project Status
Sample campaign developed for educational and portfolio purposes.

This project showcases my interests in sports marketing,
